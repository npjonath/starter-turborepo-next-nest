# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, empathetic, and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and boundaries.
- Take responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report abusive or unacceptable behavior to the maintainers using the contact
details on their GitHub profiles. You can also use
[GitHub's content reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/reporting-content-for-legal-reasons).
Do not start a public confrontation or disclose sensitive personal information
in an issue.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and privately where possible. They may
remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a contributor. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this
Code of Conduct may be removed from the project team.
